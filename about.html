Chartered Accountant | Finance, Taxation & Business Advisory

CA Kangana Sharma

CA Kangana Sharma qualified as a Chartered Accountant in 2014 and Masters of Commerce in 2018. Her education brings diverse professional experience across audit, taxation, indirect tax litigation and Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A).

Over the years, she has developed a strong understanding of financial management, regulatory compliance, tax matters and business performance across a wide range of industries, including real estate, manufacturing, service providers and trading businesses.

Her professional expertise combines technical financial knowledge with a practical, business-oriented approach. She understands the importance of accurate financial reporting, effective tax planning, regulatory compliance and meaningful financial insights in supporting sound business decisions.

With experience spanning multiple business environments, Kangana aims to help organisations strengthen their financial processes, improve reporting accuracy, navigate complex taxation matters and make informed strategic decisions.

Core Areas:

Professional Approach

Kangana believes that finance should go beyond compliance and historical reporting. It should provide management with the clarity, insights, and confidence needed to drive business performance and sustainable growth.

Her approach focuses on combining technical expertise, financial discipline and practical business understanding to deliver value-driven financial and advisory support to organisations across industries.

What We Do

Financial Services for
Modern Businesses

From compliance to strategy — choose the services that fit where your business is today, and where you want to go.

Virtual CFO

Get the strategic financial oversight your business needs without the cost of a full-time Chief Financial Officer. As your Virtual CFO, CA Kangana Sharma becomes a key part of your leadership team — providing financial clarity, risk management, and growth-oriented decision support.

This service is ideal for startups, SMEs, and scaling businesses that need expert guidance on cash flow, fundraising, financial modelling, and board reporting, but aren't yet at the stage of hiring a full-time CFO.

Monthly Financial Reporting Cash Flow Forecasting Budgeting & Variance Analysis Investor-Ready Financials KPI Dashboard Setup Board Meeting Support Cost Optimisation Financial Risk Management

Cross-Border Accounting

Expanding internationally introduces financial complexity — multi-currency transactions, transfer pricing, FEMA compliance, and foreign entity accounting. This service is designed for Indian businesses operating abroad and foreign companies with Indian operations.

We streamline your cross-border financials so you stay compliant in every jurisdiction while maintaining a consolidated, clear picture of your global financial position.

Multi-Currency Bookkeeping FEMA Compliance Transfer Pricing Advisory Foreign Subsidiary Accounts RBI Reporting Consolidation Reporting DTAA Advisory

GST Services

Goods and Services Tax compliance is non-negotiable — and the rules change frequently. CA Kangana Sharma handles everything from initial registration to complex multi-state filings, ensuring you never miss a deadline or face a penalty.

Whether you're a new business needing GST registration or an established enterprise looking to streamline your return process and reconcile mismatches, this service covers your entire GST lifecycle.

GST Registration GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B Filing Annual Return (GSTR-9) Input Tax Credit Reconciliation GST Audit Support E-Way Bill Compliance GST Notices & Litigation Reverse Charge Mechanism

ITR Filing

Accurate, on-time Income Tax Return filing for individuals, HUFs, partnership firms, LLPs, and companies. Beyond just filing, this service includes proactive tax planning to ensure you're making the most of available deductions and structuring your income to minimise liability — legally and transparently.

No more last-minute scrambles or missed deadlines. We manage your entire ITR process end-to-end, with clear communication at every step.

ITR-1 to ITR-7 Filing Tax Planning & Advisory TDS Compliance Capital Gains Computation 80C / 80D Deduction Planning Advance Tax Calculation IT Notice Response Form 26AS Reconciliation
